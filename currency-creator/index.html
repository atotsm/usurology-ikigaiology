What this model does—and does not do

  • Uses a simplified monthly flow model with active participants, acceptance, issuance, spending and a selected shock.
  • Assumes a uniform average balance and does not model individual households, prices, supply chains, taxes, default, reserves or strategic behavior.
  • Does not estimate real-world adoption or prove causal effects.
  • Outputs are sensitive to the inputs. Change one assumption at a time and compare scenarios.
Interpretation warning: a high transaction count or velocity is not automatically wellbeing. A currency can circulate rapidly while participants remain worse off. Pair monetary indicators with access, distribution, affordability, time use, inclusion and ecological outcomes.
